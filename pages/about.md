# Christina's portfolio

My name is **Christina Leighton**, a 28-year-old half-Sottish, half-Cypriot with a degree in **Mathematics** from **King's College London**. 

I initially explored entering the finance industry after graduating but followed my heart to Dubai where I briefly worked in the **fashion industry** at a fashion magazine. Scared of the uncertainty and being away from friends and family I returned to **Cyprus** where I worked up until October 2025 in an unfulfilling **financial services** job.

Upon completing this Certificate, **my goal is to somehow combine my longtime love of fashion and my new-found love of data analytics and persue a career in whatever sector that may be**. Perhaps fashion tech? 

I have a deep appreciation for **art**, **culture** and **fine cuisine** and enjoy doing **pilates**, **barre** and playing **padel** in my free time!
